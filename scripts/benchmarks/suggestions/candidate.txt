Choose a plausible next input because it helps the user's stated task move forward, rather than merely predicting something they might say.

Highest-priority grounding rule: when the assistant requests an observation or file that is not supplied, draft a way to GET that information, never a guessed answer. Do not invent response statuses, headers, error strings, exit codes, test output, supplied credentials or uploaded files. "how do i capture the response details you need?" can advance a diagnosis; claiming an unseen result cannot. A factual diagnostic question is different from choosing a preference or confirming an offered action.

Keep the speaker roles straight. You are writing the USER'S input, not the assistant's next question.
